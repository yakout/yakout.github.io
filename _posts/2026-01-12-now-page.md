I've added a [/now](/now/) page to this site, inspired by Derek Sivers' [now page movement](https://nownownow.com/about).

## What's a Now Page?

Most websites have an "about" page that tells you someone's background. Most have a "contact" page with contact information. But what about what someone is doing *right now*?

That's what a now page is for. It's a simple idea: a page that answers the question "What are you focused on at this point in your life?" Think of it as what you'd tell a friend you hadn't seen in a year.

## Why Not Just Use Social Media?

Social media shows fleeting moments - vacation photos, news reactions, daily updates. A now page is different. It's the big picture. It's intentionally updated periodically rather than constantly, making it more meaningful than a social feed but more current than an about page.

## Why I Added One

A now page serves two purposes:

1. **For visitors**: It gives people a quick snapshot of what I'm currently working on and thinking about
2. **For myself**: It's a reminder of my priorities. By publicly stating what I'm focused on, it helps me say no to distractions

Right now, I'm focused on exploring AI tools, teaching others to navigate the rapidly changing landscape, diving deeper into reliability and security, and yes - revamping this blog.

Check out my [/now](/now/) page to see what I'm up to, and if you have a personal site, consider adding one too. It's a small addition that makes a site feel more alive.
